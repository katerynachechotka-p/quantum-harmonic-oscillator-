This project builds on standard formulations of the quantum harmonic oscillator; 
implementation and analysis are original.


This project was informed by the following resources:

**MIT OpenCourseWare - Quantum Physics I**
Spring 2013, Lectures 3, 4, 5, and 8
These lectures were used for conceptual grounding in:
- The time-independent Schrödinger equation
- Operator formalism and eigenvalue problems
- Analytic properties of the quantum harmonic oscillator
- Expectation values, parity, and uncertainty relations
MIT OpenCourseWare, Massachusetts Institute of Technology.

**Computational Physics Project: The Quantum Harmonic Oscillator**
Course project handout (PHYS-4007/5007), used as methodological guidance for:
- Finite-difference discretization of the Schrödinger equation
- Matrix representation of the Hamiltonian
- Numerical eigenvalue problems and validation against analytic solutions
- Computation of expectation values and Heisenberg uncertainty products
